# GreenLeaf Microfinance Risk Assessment Methodology

## 1. Purpose

This methodology describes how risks are identified, scored, and treated within the fictional GreenLeaf Microfinance sandbox.

## 2. Likelihood Scale

1 - Rare
2 - Unlikely
3 - Possible
4 - Likely
5 - Almost Certain

## 3. Impact Scale

1 - Negligible
2 - Minor
3 - Moderate
4 - Major
5 - Severe

## 4. Risk Calculation

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

## 5. Risk Classification

1–4: Low
5–9: Medium
10–16: High
17–25: Critical

## 6. Risk Treatment

Accept: Document and monitor an acceptable risk.
Mitigate: Reduce risk through security controls.
Avoid: Stop the activity causing the risk.
Transfer: Share certain consequences with another party.

## 7. Sources and Limitations

Sources include the GreenLeaf sandbox source code, Docker configuration, the approved ISO 27001 control mapping, and OWASP Top 10 guidance.

Scores represent educational estimates for a fictional application. Observed weaknesses are distinguished from hypothetical threats. Real organizational risk acceptance requires an authorized risk owner.
